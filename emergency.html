Basic Life Support

First Aid Quick Action Procedures

Key protocols to stabilize an injured individual before paramedics arrive.

❤️ CPR Protocol

Check responsiveness. Call 112/911. Place hands in center of chest and push hard & fast (100–120 beats/min to the beat of "Stayin' Alive").

🩸 Severe Bleeding

Apply direct firm pressure with sterile cloth. Do NOT remove embedded objects. Elevate wound if possible. Apply tourniquet if trained.

🔥 Thermal Burns

Cool burn immediately under cool running water for 10–20 minutes. NEVER apply ice, butter, or toothpastes. Cover with loose sterile wrap.

🗣️ Choking (Heimlich)

Give 5 back blows between shoulder blades. If blocked, wrap arms around waist from behind and deliver inward-upward abdominal thrusts.
